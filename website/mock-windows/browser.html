Sourdough starter, day 4

Bubbles, a tangy smell and the first real rise. Here is how to feed it today.

ER Elena Rossi · October 11, 2026 · 5 min read
DAY1234567

Today's feeding Makes about 150 g

  • Starter from day 350 g
  • Whole wheat flour25 g
  • Bread flour25 g
  • Water, lukewarm50 g

Method 10 min

  1. Keep 50 g of starter and discard the rest.
  2. Stir in the flour and water until smooth.
  3. Mark the level, leave it somewhere warm.
